About NEXUS Markets

Last updated: 24 August 2026

NEXUS Markets is a free, independent financial dashboard that brings live prices for cryptocurrencies, precious metals and the world's currencies together in one fast, clean terminal.

Our goal is simple: give traders, investors and the merely curious a single screen where they can watch Bitcoin, gold, forex majors and an economic-event calendar update in real time — without clutter, pop-ups or paywalls.

What you get

  • Live crypto prices for Bitcoin and the top digital assets, streaming tick-by-tick.
  • Precious-metals spot prices for gold, silver, platinum and palladium.
  • Forex rates for every major world currency, plus an instant currency converter.
  • Market-session hours mapped to your own local time, with live open/close countdowns.
  • An economic calendar with forecasts, live countdowns and actual results.
  • A built-in market assistant that answers questions about any live price or rate.

Where the data comes from

Prices are aggregated from reputable public market-data providers, including Coinbase for cryptocurrency, and independent exchange-rate and precious-metals feeds for forex and gold. Data refreshes automatically throughout the day.

Independence

NEXUS Markets is an information service only. We are not a broker, an exchange or a financial adviser, and we never hold client funds or execute trades. The site is supported by advertising, which keeps it free for everyone.
